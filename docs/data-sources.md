# Data Sources

This repository should remain source-aware. Not every sentence needs a citation, but guide maintainers should know which sources are appropriate for which type of information.

## Preferred official sources

Use official sources for:

- Entry and visa rules
- Passport requirements
- Immigration programs
- Public health guidance
- Emergency numbers
- Travel advisories
- Public transportation systems
- Airport and rail station information
- Government services
- Tourism boards

## Useful source categories

### Government travel advice

Government travel advice can help identify safety, entry, legal, and health considerations. It should be used carefully because advisories are written for citizens of a specific country and may reflect that government’s risk tolerance.

### Immigration and border agencies

These are the best sources for entry rules and visa categories. Guides should avoid simplifying legal requirements beyond what can be safely summarized.

### Official tourism boards

Tourism boards are useful for destination context, regions, attractions, transportation links, and seasonal planning. They may be promotional, so use them as a source for factual orientation rather than subjective claims.

### Public transport agencies

Transit agencies are preferred for route, fare, pass, accessibility, and service information. Fares and schedules change, so avoid hardcoding details unless clearly dated.

### Local government sources

Local government websites may provide accessibility, public safety, emergency, event, and municipal service information.

## Source cautions

Avoid relying on a single travel blog, forum thread, or social media post for broad claims.

Avoid using outdated sources for entry rules, safety, public health, or transportation details.

Avoid copying descriptions from tourism websites. Summarize in original language and focus on practical utility.
