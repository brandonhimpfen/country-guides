# Maintenance

Travel guides become outdated unless they are reviewed regularly. This repository uses a lightweight maintenance model based on visible review dates, source-aware writing, and structural validation.

## Review frequency

Recommended review cadence:

| Section | Suggested review frequency |
| --- | --- |
| Entry and visas | Every 3 to 6 months |
| Safety and advisories | Every 3 to 6 months |
| Transportation | Every 6 to 12 months |
| Money and payments | Every 6 to 12 months |
| Connectivity | Every 6 to 12 months |
| Culture and etiquette | Every 12 months |
| Seasonal planning | Every 12 months |
| General overview | Every 12 months |

## Front matter

Every guide should include `last_reviewed`. This date should reflect the last meaningful editorial review, not a minor typo fix.

Use `status` to indicate the maturity of a guide:

- `draft`
- `reviewed`
- `needs-update`
- `archived`

## Maintenance checklist

During review, check:

- Entry and visa language
- Travel advisory context
- Major transportation changes
- Airport, rail, or transit references
- Emergency numbers
- Public health notes
- Currency and payment practices
- Accessibility notes
- Broken links
- Outdated claims
- Overly broad language

## Deprecation

If a guide cannot be maintained, mark it as `archived` rather than leaving outdated information in place.

Archived guides should include a clear note near the top explaining that the guide is no longer actively maintained.
